# Customer Onboarding & Ticket Triage SOP (Sample)

## 1. Purpose
This SOP explains how to onboard new customers and handle support tickets so each case is resolved accurately, on time, and with a clear record.

## 2. Customer Onboarding Checklist
1. Verify the customer's identity using the approved documents.
2. Confirm that the details entered match the documents.
3. Complete account setup in the system.
4. Help the customer finish activation (for example, card collection or mobile app setup).
5. Explain the next steps and how to contact support.
6. Record the outcome and flag any incomplete cases for follow-up.

## 3. Ticket Priority Matrix
| Priority | When to use | Target first response |
|---|---|---|
| High | Customer is blocked, money is affected, or a deadline applies | Within 1 hour |
| Medium | Service works but with a problem or delay | Within 4 hours |
| Low | General question or request for information | Within 24 hours |

## 4. Ticket Handling Steps
1. Read the full ticket and verify the customer.
2. Link related tickets from the same customer so nothing is duplicated.
3. Assign a priority using the matrix above.
4. Resolve the issue, or escalate if it is outside your authority.
5. Reply in clear, polite language and state the next step.
6. Follow up until the customer confirms the issue is resolved.
7. Close the ticket and record the resolution.

## 5. Escalation Rules
- Escalate when a ticket involves suspected fraud, a system fault, or a decision above your authority.
- Include in the escalation: customer verification status, a short summary, steps already taken, and the priority level.
- Record who the case was handed to and when.
- Follow up on escalated tickets until the customer receives a confirmed answer.

## 6. Sample Ticket Triage
| Ticket (fictional) | Priority | Action |
|---|---|---|
| "My card was declined and I need to pay a bill today." | High | Verify, check the account, resolve or escalate within 1 hour. |
| "How do I update my phone number?" | Low | Send the steps and close after confirmation. |
| "I was charged twice for the same item." | High | Verify, link related tickets, escalate to the billing team. |
